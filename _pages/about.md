Hi, this is **Michal**! 

Currently I am a postdoctoral researcher in Applied Machine Learning at the Centre for Digital Health Interventions at ETH, Zürich, working on the detection of driving impairment and digital biomarkers of drug intoxication.

Previously, I was a doctoral researcher at the University of Applied Sciences (SUPSI) in Lugano, doing applied research in the sleep medicine field. Before, I worked as a researcher and data scientist at [SCCH](https://www.scch.at/) in Austria. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from University of Bern, a MSc in Statistics and Data Science from [Johannes Kepler University](https://www.jku.at/institut-fuer-angewandte-statistik/) in Linz, and a Bc in Mathematics-Economics from Palacký University in Olomouc, Czechia. I come from Nový Jičín, Czechia.

Besides academia, I work as a **freelance data science consultant** for academic, industrial, and life-science clients. Among others, I lead the AI development at [Farmtec a.s.](https://www.farmtec.cz/) in the dairy industry, working on lactation-curve modelling and estrus/calving detection from wearable neck sensors. If you are looking for help with statistical analysis, ML/AI implementation, or turning your data into something useful, feel free to reach out - I am always happy to discuss a new problem.
